Employee Sentiment Analysis & Workforce Intelligence

Employees share free-text feedback. A classical machine-learning model (TF-IDF features and Logistic Regression, trained with scikit-learn) estimates whether each comment is positive, neutral or negative, and HR sees privacy-protected trends by department and over time.

Privacy by design

  • Anonymous submissions never reveal the employee to HR.
  • Groups smaller than a minimum size are hidden from sentiment breakdowns.
  • Employees only ever see their own history. Exports and feedback views are audit-logged.

Honest about limitations

  • Predictions are statistical estimates and can be wrong (sarcasm, mixed opinions, unfamiliar wording).
  • The bundled model is trained on synthetic demonstration data; retrain on real labelled feedback.
  • Sentiment is not a diagnosis and must not be used alone to judge or discipline anyone.
